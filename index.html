What this bot does

  • Answers from the clinic's own information (prices, hours, policies) — no made-up answers.
  • Checks real availability and books appointments without double-booking.
  • Saves leads (name, phone, email) for people who aren't ready to book.
  • Hands the conversation to a human when asked or when it doesn't know.
  • Can run on a website, WhatsApp or Telegram, with OpenAI, Claude or Gemini.
